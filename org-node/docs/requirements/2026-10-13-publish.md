# Requirements — a Persona's keys are its own (expectation, to be resolved later)

Added 2026-10-13 by change `worktree-org-io-publish` on the owner's ruling
of that day (plan `docs/plans/2026-10-08-org-io-publish.md` §6a, ruling 4 of
the second set). Assessed in
`org-node/docs/risk/2026-10-13-publish.md`.

**REQ-and2gz**: The software shall rely on the person unit to guarantee that
no key a Persona proposes for a membership — its Member-as-a-group key and
its Device key — is a key of the individual's Person or a key of any of the
individual's other memberships.
expects: person
opened: 2026-10-13
satisfies: derived

Why an expectation and not org-node's own requirement. Today every Persona's
seeds are freshly generated (`create_persona`), so no reuse happens, but
nothing checks it, and org-node cannot: it sees one Persona at a time and
none of the individual's other memberships or their Person's keys. The
check belongs to a unit above both — the person unit today, or a future
person-node holding the individual's Personas — which is why this is
recorded as org-node's expectation on `person` (owner, 2026-10-13: "Probably
a higher unit (e.g. a future person-node) enforces it"). It is reported
UNMET-EXPECTATION until a providing unit exports a requirement that
satisfies it; the roadmap carries it as a later row
(`docs/plans/2026-10-06-org-io-roadmap.md`).

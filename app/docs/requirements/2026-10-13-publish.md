user each Organisation's result — in step, committed, behind, removed,

From org-io's startup-reconcile requirement ("at start for every
Organisation it holds … and whenever the app asks"): org-io reconciles when
it is asked, and this is the asking. A chain that is not configured, or
cannot be read, is shown as unverified with org-io's reason, never as in
step: the app passes org-io's result through and adds no verdict of its own.

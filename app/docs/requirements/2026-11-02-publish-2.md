**REQ-yvzef9**: The software shall show, for each Organisation it holds, in
a panel titled for the Admin, each Device org-io lists as removed, by its
MemberId and DevicePublicKey, as either "acknowledged its removal at epoch
N" or "no acknowledgement received by this Device", with the statement that
an acknowledgement is the removed Device's own signed statement and not proof
that its data was deleted; and shall read that list again when the panel is
shown, when the user asks, and after every verification result it receives
for that Organisation. (implements: RC-z8cptv)
satisfies: derived

The app checks neither role itself (`app/docs/CONTEXT.md`, *Operator*): the
panel is titled "Removed Devices (Admin)", as the other Admin panels are, and
is shown to every Operator. What it lists is what this Device's own store
keeps: an acknowledgement sent to another Member's Device is not shown here.
In the single-Admin stage the Admin's Device sends every removing epoch, so
the removed Device's first recipient is the Admin's (org-node's delivering-Device requirement).

*Not in this requirement (owner ruling Q2 of 2026-11-03, as recommended):* org-io's
acknowledgement report (`OrgIo::acknowledgement_report`), a removed Device's own delivery state,
gets no app requirement in this change.

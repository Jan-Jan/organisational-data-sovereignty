# app — risk assessment of reconcile (stage S3b-io, part 1)

Change `worktree-org-io-publish`, task T15b of
`docs/plans/2026-10-08-org-io-publish.md`. Assesses the derived requirements
of `app/docs/requirements/2026-10-13-publish.md`.

**REQ-363366** (reconcile every held Organisation at start and on request;
show each result and each Organisation's verification status): it bears on
HAZ-9fmhm4, the verification display misrepresenting the verification state,
and reduces it. Before this change the Organisation list showed a record's
epoch and root with nothing to say whether the chain had confirmed them, so a
device that could not reach the chain showed its last record exactly as one
that had just verified it. Now each listed Organisation carries the status
org-io's last reconcile left — verified, unverified with the reason, or not
checked — and an unreachable or unconfigured chain is shown as unverified,
never as in step. The app adds no verdict: each result and status is
org-io's, carried across IPC unchanged in meaning (LLR-ekz4zn, LLR-8axx7h,
LLR-hwcj9n). The reconcile itself, its read and its no-deletion rule are
org-io's and assessed in org-io's risk ledger. No new hazard.

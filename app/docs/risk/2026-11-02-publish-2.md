## An acknowledgement read as proof of erasure

**HAZ-2hqt34**: the removed Devices view shows a Removal acknowledgement,
which the removed Device signs itself, and the Admin reads it as proof that a
lost or stolen Device deleted the Organisation's data, or reads the absence
of one as proof that the Device still holds it; the Admin forgoes measures
outside the app (telling the Member, revoking access the Organisation grants
elsewhere) or takes needless ones; data a thief kept stays exposed for
longer. Severity: S2. Probability: P2.

S2, not S3: the view changes no Membership record and no key; a removed
Device already holds no key of a later epoch, so the harm is the delay of a
measure outside the app, not new access. P2: an Admin who removes a stolen
Device and then sees "acknowledged" is the ordinary reader of this panel.
The same misreading is org-node's forged-acknowledgement hazard's last clause ("the
Organisation believes a revoked Device cleared that did not"), here through
the view rather than through a wrong record.

**RC-z8cptv**: the removed Devices view never states that a removed Device's
data was deleted: an acknowledged Device reads "acknowledged its removal at
epoch N", with the panel's statement that an acknowledgement is the removed
Device's own signed statement and not proof that its data was deleted; a
Device with none reads "no acknowledgement received by this Device", which
says where, not whether, it acknowledged.
mitigates: HAZ-2hqt34

Priority order: inherent safety by design is the wording itself (no text
the view shows asserts deletion or retention), backed by information for
safety (the statement). A protective measure (blocking an action on an
unacknowledged Device) has nothing to block: no app action depends on it.

What this control breaks: nothing; it fixes the view's words.

**Residual risk.** S2 / P1 with the control: an Admin who ignores the
statement. Acceptable under the matrix (S2 needs a control at P2 or worse);
acceptable, as the owner ruled on 2026-11-03 (*owner ruling Q5*, accepting
the analyst's recommendation as drafted).

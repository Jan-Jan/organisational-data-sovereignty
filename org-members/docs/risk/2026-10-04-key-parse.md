# Risk — key and device-set constructors under the parse rule

## Derived requirements assessment

assesses: LLR-k6dhz7

LLR-k6dhz7 gives `P2pMemberKey` and `P2pDeviceKey` a constructor from 32
bytes that accepts exactly what their deserialisation already accepted, so no
value enters by the new route that could not enter by the old one, and no
hazard is added. It does not refuse small-order or non-canonically encoded
keys: by owner ruling (2026-10-04) the constructors change no acceptance, and
refusing those keys is PR-b7khyw, open. (Amended 2026-10-05: this pointed at
the type-safety change's own report, which was narrowed to org-node's
non-strict signature check and moved to org-node's ledger once PR-b7khyw, the
same key-acceptance anomaly, reached master.) The residual on HAZ-bmv7cy (a serialised record carrying
a member the software would refuse) is unchanged by this item.

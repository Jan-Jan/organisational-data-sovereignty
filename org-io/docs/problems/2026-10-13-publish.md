**PR-924ftr**: the app never retries the commit of a provisional update whose chain write timed out and then executed, and offers no way to discard one: `submit_commit_send` is the only caller of org-node's `commit_update`, which commits whichever kept update matches the chain root, so the next admission can commit the earlier, timed-out update and send it to the new recipient under the new invite identifier; and every failed founding leaves another genesis update holding an Organisation private key, because nothing calls `discard_provisional`.

resolution: 2026-10-13 (change worktree-org-io-publish, tasks T14, T15, T15d; owner rulings D1, D3 of 2026-10-08 and ruling 4 of 2026-10-13) — root cause: a write whose outcome was unknown was never settled, so the next submission's commit picked up whichever kept update matched the chain, and no path discarded a kept update; fixed in three halves: (1) the late write is settled before any second write for the Organisation, committed and sent to its own submission's recipient (REQ-by9k5q; reproduced by `org-io/tests/publish_path.rs` `a_write_that_lands_after_its_timeout_is_committed_and_sent_to_its_own_recipient`), and likewise by the reconcile the app asks for (`reconcile_held`), which clears the unknown write, after which the next submission is written (`publish_path.rs` `a_reconcile_that_commits_an_unknown_write_sends_it_to_its_recipient_and_clears_it`, `after_a_reconcile_commits_an_unknown_write_the_next_submission_is_written`, `a_settle_that_finds_its_write_already_committed_lets_the_submission_write`); (2) the settle retries instead of locking the Organisation out (`publish_path.rs` `an_unknown_write_blocks_a_second_write_for_that_organisation_only`, `an_unknown_write_whose_chain_moved_elsewhere_is_reconciled`, `a_failed_read_leaves_the_outcome_unknown_and_changes_nothing`, `within_the_window_the_outcome_stays_unknown`, `past_the_window_at_the_records_epoch_the_write_is_not_executed`, `a_settle_that_finds_no_state_or_an_older_state_stays_unknown`, `a_settle_whose_record_is_no_longer_held_is_refused`, `a_committed_late_write_whose_send_fails_keeps_the_commit`); (3) "no way to discard" is answered by the background discard (REQ-dqbg9p as rewritten 2026-10-13: every commit drops the Organisation's other provisional updates, a write known not executed is discarded by its settle, a founding drops the Persona's other genesis updates;

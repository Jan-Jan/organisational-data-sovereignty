task behind it, and the plan rules these items "amended in place", so the

`verifies:` one of these IDs no longer says which wording it was written

opposite rules is meant without reading its notes; the checklist's rule
exists to prevent exactly that, most of all for a reversed meaning.

The decision for the owner: either confirm amendment in place with dated,
quoting notes as this project's practice (and have the checklist say so,
with a limit such as "a reversal of meaning is always superseded"), or
supersede the items above — at least an earlier form of REQ-uk9rw7 — in a follow-up change,
with new IDs, `supersedes:`/`superseded-by:` pairs and the tests
re-annotated.

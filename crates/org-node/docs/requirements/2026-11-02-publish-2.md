# org-node: the acknowledged record and the delivering Device (S3b-io part 2)

Change `worktree-org-io-publish-2`, task T13b as rescoped by its §0 (owner
rulings R1–R4 of 2026-11-02). Written 2026-11-03 against master `7ac0825`
(domain-api merged). Two capabilities: a Member keeps the Removal
acknowledgements it accepts (plan task T16g, ruling 5 of 2026-10-16 as cut

The owner was not asked anything in this task. Items that rested on a
recommendation name the owner's ruling on it: the owner ruled on the plan's
§4 questions (Q1–Q6) on 2026-11-03, each as recommended.

## The acknowledged record (T16g)

**REQ-h7y5ur**: The software shall keep, in the Persona store of a Member,
each Removal acknowledgement it accepts from a Device it holds as revoked,
the first one accepted from each such Device and no other from it, in the
order accepted; shall never remove or change a kept one while the store is
not removed; and shall delete them with the rest of the store's data only on
that Persona's own removal.
satisfies: derived

Ruling 5 of 2026-10-16, as §0 of the plan cuts it down (2026-11-02): the
shared revoked-device list of roadmap stage S5 is out of scope, and in its
place each Device keeps an append-only record of the acknowledgements it
accepted, never shared. **TODO (roadmap S5):** the S5 revoked-device CRDT
replaces this record; the code carries the same TODO.

Accepting an acknowledgement is unchanged (REQ-b462sh): a Member still
accepts one from a Device its current record does not list, whether or not
it holds that Device as revoked. Only the keeping is limited, to Devices held
as revoked and to one per Device, so that the record grows only with the
removals this Member adopted and no Device, revoked or never listed, can grow
it by signing more acknowledgements (*owner ruling Q3 of 2026-11-03*: an
acknowledgement from a Device not held as revoked is accepted and nothing is
kept; it is not refused).

**REQ-f8pu8a**: The software shall return, for an Organisation whose current
record a Persona store holds, every Device that store holds as revoked, with
the MemberId it was listed under and the acknowledgement kept from it, if
any, changing nothing.
satisfies: derived

The value org-io passes to the app for the Admin's view of removed Devices
(org-io's and the app's T16g requirements). "Held as revoked" is LLR-bgj2ff's
rule: listed by a record this store replaced and not listed by the current
one.

## The delivering Device (T16c4)

**REQ-32cp7f**: The software shall, on its own removal from an Organisation,
choose as the first recipient of its Removal acknowledgements the Device it
received the removal from — the sender of the Organisation information or
the organisation answer whose epoch verification matched, or the sender of
the revocation notice verification accepted, whether pushed or answering this
Device's organisation request — whenever REQ-6tepxq's count is not zero and
that Device is not this Persona's own; and shall choose the others, up to
that count, from the other Devices its record held before the removal lists.
satisfies: derived

Owner ruling R2 (2026-11-02). REQ-6tepxq still sets how many acknowledgements
are kept; this item rules which Device is first, the question REQ-6tepxq left
open (its dated note of 2026-11-03). The sender rule (LLR-283x6f) accepts
Organisation information, an organisation answer and a revocation notice only
from a Device the current record lists, so the delivering Device is always
one of REQ-6tepxq's candidates and the count is unchanged. Whether the
delivering Device can be reached is the addresses' question, which org-io's
T16c4 requirement answers (*owner ruling Q1 (c) of 2026-11-03*: in
Networked mode, a dial by key when no usable address is kept).
